\subsection{Prototype Decision-Path Cost on the Target}
\label{sec:hardware}

The study ran on a \cfive{module} with \cfive{jetpack} in the \cfive{powermode} power mode, with OpenPLC~\cfive{openplc} in the \cfive{topology} topology and a 10~ms task period. Every worker ran pinned to one verified core, and every raw sample was retained. Preflight measured a \cfive{timerres}~ns timer resolution, detected all \cfive{mutants} single-stage disabled-check mutants, and confirmed fail-closed decisions on all \cfive{controls} negative controls.

Fig.~\ref{fig:hardware} separates the cost components. Disabling the audit enqueue changed the median by \cfive{auditcost}~$\mu$s; this and the unsigned diagnostic are not deployable configurations. A Redis revocation lookup pinned on the target had a worst p99 of \cfive{remoteworst}~$\mu$s, and a revocation became visible \cfive{proppfifty}~$\mu$s after issuance at the median (p99 \cfive{proppninetynine}~$\mu$s). On the common expressible subset, pinned SINT had a worst p99 of \cfive{sintworstms}~ms. That is \cfive{ratiofull} times the full \zonecap{} path and \cfive{ratiomiss} times its cache-cleared variant. The ratio is not a like-for-like speedup. SINT verifies only its leaf token, with a JavaScript Ed25519 library on every request, and its timed call contains no context or audit stage. No executable PLC ABAC/NGAC artifact was available. \ifcsname cfive@etoepninetynine\endcsname End-to-end, from client send to OpenPLC acknowledgement, the median was \cfive{etoepfifty}~ms and p99 was \cfive{etoepninetynine}~ms, including up to one scan period of phase wait.
